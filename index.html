FAQ

Common questions.

Do you clean outside our business hours?

Yes — most of our commercial cleaning is done after hours or before opening, so your team and customers are never in the way. We build the schedule around your operating hours.

Are you insured?

We carry public liability insurance and can provide a certificate of currency on request before any work begins.

Will you keep our site confidential?

Absolutely. We work discreetly and are happy to sign an NDA for clients in health, legal, government or other sensitive settings.

What areas do you service?

We service [SERVICE AREA]. If you're not sure whether you're in our area, send us your suburb and we'll confirm.

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Yes — we bring professional-grade products and equipment, including hospital-grade disinfectants and HEPA-filtration vacuums. Eco-conscious options are available on request.

Do you do once-off cleans, or only ongoing contracts?

Both. We take on ongoing after-hours contracts as well as one-off end of lease / vacate (make-good) cleans for commercial tenants.

How do I get a quote?

Send us your site details through the contact form or give us a call. We'll assess what's needed and come back with clear, upfront pricing — no obligation.
