FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you might want to know about working with Doyle Cleaning Co. Can't find your answer? Get in touch — we're happy to help.

Services & scheduling

What kind of cleaning do you do?

We're a commercial cleaning business with two core services: ongoing after-hours cleaning for offices, retail and medical spaces, and one-off end of lease / vacate (make-good) cleans for commercial tenants.

Do you clean outside our business hours?

Yes — most of our commercial cleaning is done after hours or before opening, so your team and customers are never in the way. We build the schedule around your operating hours.

Do you do once-off cleans, or only ongoing contracts?

Both. We take on ongoing after-hours contracts as well as one-off end of lease / vacate cleans. Tell us what you need and we'll recommend the right approach.

How often can you clean?

Daily, weekly, fortnightly or a custom schedule — whatever suits your site and budget.

What types of sites do you clean?

Offices and professional services, medical and allied-health rooms, retail and hospitality venues, warehouses and industrial units, strata and real-estate spaces, and fitness and wellness studios.

Trust, insurance & compliance

Are you insured?

We carry public liability insurance and can provide a certificate of currency on request before any work begins.

Are your staff background-checked?

Anyone attending a site holds a current National Police Check and any clearances a site requires. As we grow, this remains a firm requirement for every team member.

Will you keep our site and information confidential?

Yes. We work discreetly and are happy to sign an NDA for clients in health, legal, government or other sensitive settings.

Who actually does the cleaning?

We're owner-operated — the people who quote your job are the people accountable for it, from the first walkthrough to the final inspection.

Products & standards

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Yes — we bring professional-grade products and equipment, including hospital-grade disinfectants and HEPA-filtration vacuums.

Do you use hospital-grade / medical-safe products?

Yes. For medical and allied-health sites we use TGA-registered, hospital-grade disinfectants and colour-coded microfibre to prevent cross-contamination between areas.

Do you offer eco-friendly cleaning?

We can. Low-tox, Australian-made and eco-certified product options are available on request.

Can you provide Safety Data Sheets (SDS)?

Yes — Safety Data Sheets for the products we use are available on request.

Quotes & getting started

How do I get a quote?

Send us your site details through the contact form or give us a call. We'll assess what's needed and come back with clear, upfront pricing — no obligation.

What areas do you service?

We service [SERVICE AREA]. If you're not sure whether you're in our area, send us your suburb and we'll confirm.

How much does commercial cleaning cost?

Every site is different — size, frequency and the type of space all matter — so we quote individually rather than list a flat rate. You'll always get a clear price before we start.

Do you offer a re-clean if something's missed?

For end of lease / vacate cleans, we'll return to re-clean any areas flagged at inspection, on terms confirmed in writing with your quote.
