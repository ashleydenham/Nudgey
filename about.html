About Us

Experienced operators, high standards.

Doyle Cleaning Co is an owner-operated commercial cleaning business. Our founders bring professional backgrounds in healthcare-facilities management, compliance and hospitality — and apply that same discipline to every clean.

Our Approach

Run like a professional operation — because it is.

We started Doyle Cleaning Co to bring a higher, more accountable standard to commercial cleaning. Because the owners carry out and oversee the work personally, clients get consistency, discretion and a documented process rather than a rotating roster of unfamiliar contractors. Every site is cleaned to a customised checklist, with regular inspections so standards hold over time.
