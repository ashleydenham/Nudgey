About Us

Experienced operators, high standards.

Doyle Cleaning Co is an owner-operated commercial cleaning business. Our founders bring professional backgrounds in healthcare-facilities management, compliance and hospitality — and apply that same discipline to every clean.

Our Approach

Run like a professional operation — because it is.

We started Doyle Cleaning Co to bring a higher, more accountable standard to commercial cleaning. Because the owners carry out and oversee the work personally, clients get consistency, discretion and a documented process rather than a rotating roster of unfamiliar contractors. Every site is cleaned to a customised checklist, with regular inspections so standards hold over time. And we don't just manage the work — both of us are hands-on cleaners, on the tools ourselves.

Our Story

It started at home.

Ashley and Bradley are a Brighton couple — soon to be married — who happen to be the kind of people who argue over who gets to do the cleaning. We love making a space organised, spotless and just-so, and one day it clicked: why not build a business around the thing we already can't help doing?

We're family-oriented and in it for the long term. Doyle Cleaning Co is the first of the family businesses we want to build together — and we bring the same care to a client's site that we bring to our own home.

We're a proudly LGBTQI+ and neurodivergent-owned business, and we think that eye for detail and genuine care is exactly what you want in the people looking after your space.
