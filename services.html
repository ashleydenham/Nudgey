Who We Clean For

Three kinds of client. On purpose.

We’re not trying to clean everything in Adelaide. We focus on the commercial sites where our two backgrounds — healthcare facilities and hospitality — actually show in the result.

Offices & professional suites

Desks, meeting rooms, kitchens and bathrooms done overnight, so the first person in flicks the lights on to a finished job. Discretion as standard; NDAs where the work needs it.

Cafés & restaurants

Front of house presented like it matters and back of house degreased like a kitchen inspection is tomorrow. Brad ran hospitality teams — we clean to the standard he used to enforce.

The Fine Print

Our service terms, in plain English.

The full terms arrive in writing with your quote. This is the honest summary — the way we’d explain it across a table.

01The job sheet is the scope

Your quote follows a free walkthrough, and the written checklist we build there is the contract of work. If it’s on the sheet, it gets done every visit. Anything extra is quoted before we touch it — never billed as a surprise.

02Ongoing contracts, no traps

An agreed schedule, invoiced monthly on [14-day] terms. No long lock-ins: either side can end the agreement with [30 days’] written notice. Pricing is reviewed [annually], and never changes without notice in writing.

03One-off jobs, fixed quotes

Specific jobs — vacate cleans, pre-opening cleans, deep cleans — get a fixed written quote before we start. Vacate cleans include a re-clean of any areas flagged at inspection, on the terms confirmed with your quote.

04Access & security

Keys and alarm codes are logged, stored securely, and returned the day an agreement ends. We work happily under your CCTV, and body-worn cameras are available by agreement for a full record.

05If something goes wrong

Breakages and incidents are reported to you the same night — you hear it from us, not from your staff the next morning. Public liability insurance is in place, with a certificate of currency provided with every contract.

06Confidentiality

What we see on your site stays on your site. NDAs are available for legal, financial and other sensitive environments, and our staff obligations mirror whatever we sign with you.
